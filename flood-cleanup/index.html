A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, North Charleston Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout North Charleston to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation
  • Keep documents and keepsakes in watertight bins on shelves, not on a basement floor
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in North Charleston

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
