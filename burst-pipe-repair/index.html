Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

When a pipe bursts in North Charleston or the surrounding area, North Charleston Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Install water leak detection sensors near water heaters and main supply lines
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts

The Benefits of Professional Burst Pipe Repair

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.

When to Call for Burst Pipe Repair in North Charleston

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
